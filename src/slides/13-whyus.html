Act V · The case

Why Officebanao

01

We are the user.

The cycle in Act I — Design, Model, Documentation, BOQ, MEP, Site Execution — is Officebanao’s own process.

The pain is first-hand, not researched.

02

The scope is the moat.

A dedicated tool, not a general-purpose BIM platform. Built only for office interiors.

Revit carries generalised complexity because it has to handle anything from a bridge to a hospital.

A tool scoped that narrowly can only be built by someone whose work is scoped that narrowly.

03

The demand already exists.

Firms that want BIM but find it too complicated.

Firms on BIM who find it too rigid to sustain.

The two firm types from Act II are already looking for something else.

This slide is the strategic argument only — it makes no market-size or revenue claim.
